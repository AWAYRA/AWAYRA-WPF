# Support Awayra

Awayra is free and open-source software.

If Awayra helps you, you can optionally support its continued development. Support is completely optional and never unlocks features.

## Primary support link

https://www.buymeacoffee.com/YOUR_USERNAME

## Payment handling

Awayra does not process payment data. When you choose to support the project, your browser opens the external funding provider's website, where any payment is handled entirely by that provider under their own terms and privacy policy.

Awayra does not store payment information, track donations, or gate features based on support.
