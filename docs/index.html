A calmer reminder for your eyes and body.

Awayra combines independent eye and movement timers, stable fullscreen overlays, optional locally generated sounds, and guided break exercises without accounts, subscriptions, ads, telemetry, or cloud services.

Designed for long computer sessions.

Awayra is a native .NET and WPF application for developers, office workers, designers, students, gamers, and anyone who needs consistent screen and movement breaks.

Two independent timers

Configure Eye Reset and Move Break intervals and durations separately.

Stable fullscreen overlays

Monitor wake, unlock, resume, and display changes are stabilized before repositioning.

Guided exercises

An animated eye exercise with ten counted blinks, and a movement routine that walks you through standing, stretching, and returning.

Flexible controls

Pause, snooze, skip, complete, or start either reminder immediately.

Smart idle handling

Reset reminders after idle time and optionally restrict them to work hours.

Private and offline

Settings, state, statistics, and logs stay on the local computer. Awayra makes no network calls.

Gentle offline sound options.

Choose Soft bell, Gentle chime, Calm drop, or the original Awayra Calm piano loop. Every waveform is generated locally by the application; no third-party music file is downloaded or bundled.
