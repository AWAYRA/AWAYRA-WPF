A practical break routine.

Awayra keeps eye resets and movement breaks independent, so each reminder can use the right interval and duration.

20–20–20

Eye Reset

Every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet or 6 metres away for 20 seconds and blink naturally.

30–60

Move Break

Every 30 to 60 minutes, stand up for at least one minute, walk briefly, and release static posture.

Privacy is not an option you need to enable.

Awayra stores settings, timer state, statistics, and diagnostic logs locally on your computer. It does not upload screenshots, browsing history, application activity, or personal information.

Frequently asked questions.

Is Awayra free?

Yes. Awayra is free and open-source software licensed under GPL-3.0-only.

Does Awayra follow the 20-20-20 rule?

Yes. Its default eye reminder runs every 20 minutes for 20 seconds. All reminder intervals are configurable.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Awayra works locally after installation and does not require an account or online service.

Does it collect data?

No. Awayra does not send telemetry or personal data to a server.

Where is the official download?

Use the direct installer link on this page or the GitHub Releases page. Executables in branches or third-party mirrors are not official releases.

Is it available in the Microsoft Store?

No. Awayra is distributed directly through GitHub Releases.
